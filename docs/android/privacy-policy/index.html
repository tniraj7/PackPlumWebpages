Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

PackPlum for Android keeps the profiles, timetables, contacts, and photos you enter on your device, without an account, cloud sync, or Android backup. Firebase Crashlytics sends crash and error diagnostics to help us fix problems; these do not include what you enter. Timetable scanning reads photos on your device. PackPlum is free and shows family-friendly, non-personalized ads from Google AdMob. The developer does not collect your profiles or timetables, sell personal data, or track you across apps or websites.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the PackPlum Android app (application ID com.codecat15.packplum) handles information. PackPlum for iPhone and iPad works a little differently and has its own privacy policy. PackPlum is developed and maintained by codecat15 (“the developer,” “we,” or “us”).

2. Information you add to PackPlum

PackPlum asks only for a profile name. Everything else is optional and entered by you:

  • Profiles: a display name, an optional class, year, or semester label, and an optional profile photo.
  • Activities: school, college, tuition, or extra-class schedules with their names, start times, and weekdays, and an optional institution name and address that you type in.
  • Timetables and packing: subjects, books and other items, weekly timetables and drafts, extra items, holidays and cancelled days, and which items you have ticked off for a date.
  • Contacts: optional contacts such as an emergency contact, teacher, tutor, or classmate, with a label, name, phone number, and email address. You can type these or pick one from your device's contacts.
  • Device settings: your packing reminder time and days, appearance, the selected profile, and whether you have finished the welcome.

PackPlum does not ask for a date of birth, school login, email address for an account, or your location. This information is used on your device to build each day's bag, show timetables, and schedule your reminder. It is not sent to the developer or to a PackPlum server; there is no PackPlum server.

3. Data collection and tracking

Crash diagnostics: PackPlum uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes, app-not-responding events, and some handled errors. Reporting is on automatically; the app does not currently offer a switch to turn it off. Reports can include the error type and stack trace, app version and state, device model and Android version, timestamps, and installation and session identifiers used to measure app stability. For handled errors, PackPlum removes the original error message and sends only a fixed label for where it happened. PackPlum does not attach profiles, names, timetables, contacts, addresses, or photos to any report, and does not set a user ID in Crashlytics. Automatic crash reports are produced by the Firebase SDK and may include error details.

Google processes these diagnostics to provide Firebase, and we use them only to find and fix problems. They are sent encrypted over HTTPS. See Firebase's privacy and security information.

Text recognition metrics: timetable scanning uses Google's ML Kit, which reads photos on your device (see section 6). The photo and the text found in it are not sent to Google or the developer. Like other ML Kit features, it may contact Google to receive updates and send Google general performance and usage metrics about the feature, which Google uses to maintain and improve ML Kit. See ML Kit's terms.

Advertising: PackPlum is free and shows banner ads from Google AdMob. After you save a timetable imported from a photo, you may see a full-screen ad that you can close. It can appear at most once every five minutes. Ads load only while the device is online. The rest of PackPlum works without them.

Where the law requires it, Google's consent tool may ask for your choice before ads are shown, and PackPlum does not show ads until that check allows them.

Every ad request is marked as directed at children and users under the age of consent and is limited to family-friendly content (Google's G rating). Google therefore does not show personalized ads or remarketing, and the ads are not based on what you enter or what you do in other apps. PackPlum never gives the ad service your profiles, names, timetables, contacts, addresses, or photos.

To deliver ads, Google's ad software in the app may receive your device's IP address (which can show its approximate location), device and app details such as model, operating system, and app version, and information about the ads shown and tapped. Google uses this to show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them, and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

PackPlum does not request Android's advertising ID permission, so neither PackPlum nor Google's ad software can read your device's advertising ID.

When your region requires it, Ad privacy options on PackPlum's Data & privacy screen opens Google's form so you can review or change your choice.

PackPlum does not include Google Analytics, does not use your location, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

When you open this website: like any website, the server that hosts this page receives your IP address and basic request details, such as the page requested and your browser type, and may keep them in server logs to deliver and protect the site. This website does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising. Your theme choice is remembered only in your own browser. Opening this page does not send anything you entered in PackPlum.

4. Storage and security

Everything you enter is stored in PackPlum's private storage on your device. Android keeps this storage private to PackPlum, so other apps cannot read it.

Android backup is turned off for PackPlum. Its data is not copied to your Google Account's backup and does not sync to other devices or any cloud service. PackPlum also asks Android to leave its data out of device-to-device transfers; some manufacturers' transfer tools may not follow this. Because nothing is backed up, uninstalling PackPlum or losing your device removes its data. Crash diagnostics are separate from your data and are processed by Firebase.

Profile photos you choose or take are copied into PackPlum as a smaller, upright image without the original photo's metadata, such as where it was taken.

No security method can prevent every loss or unauthorized access. You are responsible for keeping control of your device and its screen lock.

5. Device access and permissions

PackPlum does not request camera, photo, storage, contacts, or location permissions. It uses device features only when you choose an action that needs them:

  • Camera: Take photo opens your device's camera app. PackPlum receives only the photo you take and confirm, for a profile picture or to fill a timetable.
  • Photos: Android's photo picker gives PackPlum only the image you select.
  • Contacts: Android's contact picker gives PackPlum only the name and phone number of the entry you select. PackPlum keeps only what you save and does not read your address book.
  • Notifications: to show your optional packing reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device and say only “Time to pack tomorrow's bag”, without names, contacts, or bag contents. PackPlum also restores your reminder after the device restarts, the app updates, or the time or time zone changes.
  • Internet: lets Firebase send crash diagnostics, lets ML Kit work, and loads ads, as described above. Android grants this without a prompt. PackPlum's own features work offline.

You can manage notification permission in Android Settings. Turning it off only stops reminders.

6. How timetable scanning works

PackPlum can fill in a timetable from a photo of a printed or handwritten timetable. Every step happens on your device:

  • You choose the photo. Take one with the camera, or pick one from your photos.
  • PackPlum finds the table. It straightens the photo and works out where the days, periods, and cells are.
  • The text is read on your device. Google's ML Kit text recognition, built into the app reads the words in each cell. PackPlum also reads any times printed on the timetable, but only to work out which cell belongs to which period. Times are not saved.
  • You check the result. PackPlum shows the subjects it found for each day so you can correct, remove, or add anything. Nothing is saved yet.
  • You save. Only the subjects you approve are added to the timetable. A photo you take for scanning is held briefly in PackPlum's private cache and deleted after reading; a photo you pick is read in place. PackPlum does not keep either.

PackPlum never uploads the photo or the text read from it, never sends them to the developer or an online text recognition or AI service, and never uses them to train AI models. Scanning works in airplane mode.

ML Kit may send Google general usage metrics about the feature, as described in section 3. These never include the photo or its text.

Recognition can misread handwriting, glare, or blurry photos. That's why you always review the result before saving.

7. Contacts, sharing, and disclosure

PackPlum never calls, messages, emails, or sends reminders to your saved contacts on its own. When you tap a saved phone number or email address and choose an action, PackPlum hands only that number or address to the app you pick:

  • Call opens your phone app with the number filled in.
  • Message opens your messaging app addressed to the number.
  • Email opens your mail app with a new draft to the address.
  • Share opens Android's share sheet with just the phone number, so you can send it with another app.

Nothing is sent until you send it in that app. The app you choose, and anyone you share with, handles the information under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may disclose information if you send it to us yourself, or if the law requires it. Because the developer never receives what you enter in PackPlum, we cannot access or disclose it from a PackPlum server.

8. Optional support emails

If you choose Settings → Share feedback, PackPlum opens your mail app with a new draft addressed to packplum@gmail.com and the subject “PackPlum feedback”. PackPlum adds nothing else: no profiles, timetables, logs, or device details. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email.

If you send it, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach, and use them only to reply, troubleshoot, and improve PackPlum. Please don't include a child's personal details or other sensitive information.

Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, keep support records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your messages using the contact details below; we may need enough information to find them, and legal exceptions may apply.

9. Keeping and deleting your information

Information stays in PackPlum until you delete it or uninstall the app. Deleting a profile removes its activities, timetables, subjects and items, contacts, photo, and packing history from the device and cancels its reminders. Deleting an activity removes only that activity's timetable and days off. Uninstalling PackPlum deletes all of its data from the device.

Because Android backup is off for PackPlum and we keep no server copy, the developer cannot restore deleted data or data from a lost device.

Crash diagnostics: Firebase states that Crashlytics keeps crash reports and their installation identifiers for 90 days before removing them. Other Firebase data follows Firebase's retention practices. Deleting profiles or uninstalling PackPlum does not erase diagnostics already sent. Contact us with any questions about them.

10. Children's privacy

PackPlum is made for families: parents keeping track of their children's bags, and students who manage their own. A parent or guardian may enter a child's name, class, photo, timetable, and contacts, and that information stays on the device where it was entered. PackPlum does not ask anyone's age or create accounts. Section 3 explains the separate diagnostics and advertising information processed when people use the app.

Because children use PackPlum, every ad request is marked as directed at children, so Google limits the information it collects for ads, shows only family-friendly ads, and does not personalize them or use remarketing. Section 3 describes what Google's ad software receives.

On Android, the crash diagnostics described above are collected from every installation, including a device a child uses. They describe the app and device, not the person using it, and never include what is entered in PackPlum.

Only enter another person's information, such as a teacher's phone number, when you are allowed to. If a child uses PackPlum on their own device, a parent or guardian can review and delete what is stored there at any time.

11. International use

PackPlum keeps your information on your device. Google may process crash diagnostics, ML Kit metrics, and the ad information described in section 3 outside your country under its own terms and privacy practices. If you open this website, email us, or share information through another app, that provider may process what it receives in other countries under its own privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if PackPlum's features, data practices, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will change when we publish an update, and important changes will be shared in the app or on its Google Play listing when appropriate.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email the developer at packplum@gmail.com. Please don't include a child's personal details, photos, or other sensitive information in your message.
