Private by design

Your data never leaves your device.

PackPlum holds children's names, timetables and contacts, so it's built to keep them close. Everything you enter is stored on your phone or tablet, and nowhere else. Not on our servers, because we don't have any.

Privacy Policy for iPhone & iPad Privacy Policy for Android

  • Stays on your device Profiles, timetables, contacts and photos are stored only on your phone or tablet, and left out of cloud backups.
  • No account, no servers There's nothing to sign up for and no PackPlum server to send your data to. We never see it.
  • Works offline Timetables, bags and reminders all work without an internet connection.
  • Family-friendly ads, no tracking Ads are rated for kids and never personalized. No analytics, and nothing that follows you across other apps or websites.

The ads never see anything you enter in PackPlum. Crash reports help us fix bugs by describing the app and device. PackPlum does not attach the information you enter.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is PackPlum free?

Yes. Profiles, timetables, filling a timetable from a photo, packing, holidays and reminders are all free, with no subscription. PackPlum shows a few family-friendly ads, and they're never personalized.

How does the timetable scan work?

Take or pick a photo of the timetable. PackPlum finds the table and reads each cell on your device, then shows you the subjects for each day to check and correct. The photo is never uploaded, and PackPlum doesn't keep it. The privacy policy explains each step.

Do I need to create an account?

No. PackPlum works the moment you open it. There's no sign-up, email or password.

Where is my child's information kept?

Only on your phone or tablet. Names, timetables, contacts and photos never leave the device, aren't sent to us, and are left out of cloud backups. Crash reports help us fix bugs, and PackPlum does not attach what you enter.

Can I use it for more than one child?

Yes. Add a profile for each child — or for yourself — and each one gets their own schedules, items and reminder.

My child has school and tuition. Does that work?

That's exactly what PackPlum is for. Add school, college, tuition and extra classes as separate schedules, and PackPlum combines them into one bag for each day. Items needed by both are only listed once.

What happens on holidays?

Cancel a single day for just the schedules that are off. Everything else, and the rest of your week, stays as it is.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Everything — timetables, packing and reminders — works offline.

Does it sync between my phone and my child's tablet?

Not yet. In this first release each device keeps its own data. Syncing between your Apple devices is planned for a later update.
